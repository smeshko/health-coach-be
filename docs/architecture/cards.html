is_hard ≠ day_type

A card carries two independent "hardness" flags. One budgets training; the other cycles carbs. Keeping them separate is what lets code derive both correctly.

The two flags a card carries

axiscolumndrives
Training loadis_hardhard-day budget & spacing (§5.1, §6.2) — max 2–3/wk, never back-to-back
Fuel demandday_typenutrition carb-cycling (§7.3) — hard / moderate / rest
They differ: a long_run is is_hard = false (it's the marquee easy session) but day_type = hard (you still carb-load to fuel it).
day_type → carbs (§7.3): hard ≈ 4–5 g/kg · moderate ≈ 3 g/kg · rest ≈ 2–2.5 g/kg. Protein & fat constant; carbs + calories move with day_type.
day_type here is the DEFAULT + fuel FLOOR, not fixed. The daily LLM picks the actual dayType as its one nutrition lever — it may decouple fuel from training (drop an easy day to rest carbs for a deficit push, or hold hard the day before a key session). Guardrail: a is_hard or long_run card floors dayType at hard — fuel up, never under-fuel. Grams stay code-computed.

Flags & downgrades

Flags are code-filled machine keys the app reads and the validator enforces. The downgrade map drives AMBER/RED auto-regulation (§6).

Flags vocabulary

what the validator enforces

Invariants

The validator checks picks against this table — so changing a card moves the schema, the prompt, and the validator together.

Checked against CARD_META

  • chosen card ∈ CARD_META
  • durationMin within the card's dose band
  • Weekly: count(is_hard) ≤ budgets.hardDays; no two hard cards on adjacent days; no hard run after boxing; strength count == budget; core 2–3 · extras 1–2 · ≤1 long_run
  • Daily: RED ⇒ day_type=rest card only; AMBER ⇒ no full vo2/z5 dose; knee_pain>3 ⇒ no impact card; card ∈ week plan ∪ low-impact subs; chosen dayType ≥ the card's fuel floor (is_hard/long_run ⇒ hard)
No medical denylist — §2 conditions are LLM context, not code-enforced (fitness app, not a medical device).
